Page 3 · Signals

Ten signals worth a system. Vanta-style, applied to CX.

Vanta built a monitoring system that detects companies needing SOC2 before they realise it. The same pattern, applied to Solidroad's ICP, catches CX teams the moment they need to evaluate quality infrastructure – and lets us reach them before they know what to call the category.

01 · the framework

Every signal runs through FETC.

Clay's framework, applied per signal. Find (what to detect, where). Enrich (what to add once detected). Transform (how to score / qualify). Create (the outreach hook). Same shape for all ten.

Signal categories

  • Growth signals. Companies scaling, creating new CX needs.
  • Change signals. Transitions that disrupt vendor relationships – new CX leaders, AI agent deployments.
  • Distress signals. Public CX failures, viral complaints, regulatory pressure.
  • Intent signals. LinkedIn engagement, conference attendance, content downloads.

How signals stack

A single signal alone is interesting. Multiple signals in 30 days is buying intent. Function 3 (Signal Scoring) weights ICP fit + signal strength + signal velocity. Three signals in 14 days on an ICP-fit account = call them today, not next week.

02 · the ten signals

Each one a hypothesis. Each one testable.

Signal 1 · AI agent deployed without a quality story (highest-value)

The hypothesis. Companies announcing AI chatbots / AI voicebots / AI email responders in customer support, without any mention of monitoring, evaluation, or quality infrastructure. They have a hidden quality problem that will compound; Solidroad solves it.

Find. Daily Claygent scrape of (a) earnings call transcripts for B2C-heavy companies mentioning "AI agent" or "AI support" + (b) homepage chat-widget detection (Intercom Fin, Ada, Decagon, Forethought live) + (c) press releases / SEC filings. Cross-reference with the existence of a Trust & Safety or AI Ethics page (negative signal: probably already thinking about it).

Enrich. Function 1 (Account ICP). Function 2 (Persona Mapper, targeting VP Customer Support, Head of AI, Director of CX Operations).

Transform. Score boost for: AI agent live + no public quality framework + recent CX leadership hire. This is the gold-mine triad.

Create. Outreach hook: "Saw [Company] just launched [AI agent name]. Curious how you're scoring the conversations it's handling – we work with [similar customer] on exactly this. Worth 15 minutes?"

Why this is signal #1. It's the Vanta play, structurally identical. Vanta catches companies that need SOC2 before they know what to call it. This catches companies that need Solidroad before they've started evaluating.

Signal 2 · LinkedIn engagement on Solidroad employee posts

The hypothesis. Mark already said publicly that LinkedIn is Solidroad's top growth channel, and he's paying $5K/month bonuses to amplify reach. The reach is working. The conversion mechanism is manual. We can automate the conversion and turn engagement into pipeline within 24 hours.

Find. Function 6 (LinkedIn Engagement Harvester). Daily scrape of likes / comments / reposts on posts from Mark, Patrick, Paddy, Andrew Mooney, Robbie Carroll, and any team member posting consistently.

Enrich. Function 2 (Persona Mapper) on every engager. Pull their work email, title, recent posts of their own, company.

Transform. Function 3 (Signal Scoring) per engager: ICP fit + engagement type (comment > repost > like) + recency (last 7 days = hot) + their own company's CX signals.

Create. Daily Slack digest for the operator: top 5 engagers by score, with one-click "draft outreach" button that runs Function 4 (Outreach Drafter) referencing the specific post they engaged on.

Why this is signal #2. Mark has explicitly told the market this is Solidroad's top growth channel. The reach is working. The conversion mechanism is currently manual. This Function industrialises it.

Signal 3 · New CX leader joining (Head of Support / VP CX / Director of Quality)

The hypothesis. New CX leaders within 60 days of starting are in their "make a mark" window. They have budget authority, mandate to change vendors, and incentive to ship visible wins fast. Highest single-signal conversion in B2B SaaS.

Find. Default signal: job changes at target accounts. Filter for titles matching CX leadership patterns: "Head of Support", "VP Customer Experience", "Director of CX", "Head of Quality", "VP Customer Operations".

Enrich. Function 2 on the new hire. Pull their previous-company context: did they use Solidroad's competitor? Did they speak publicly about quality, training, or AI agents at their last role?

Transform. Score boost: ICP-fit company + new hire in CX leadership + their previous role used a Solidroad competitor or wrote publicly about CX quality.

Create. Outreach hook tailored by previous role: "Saw you just joined [new company] as [title] – congrats. At [old company] you wrote about [specific thing]. We're working with [similar company] on exactly that shape of problem. Worth a quick intro?"

Signal 4 · Public CX failure (viral complaint, regulatory action, news mention)

The hypothesis. Public CX failures create internal urgency at the affected company. Within 7 days of a viral support failure or a regulatory complaint, the CX leader is being asked by their CEO / board what's being done. Solidroad's "100% review" pitch lands hardest at this exact moment.

Find. RSS feed + Claygent monitoring of: (a) Reddit r/[company] threads tagged with support complaints, (b) Twitter mentions of [company] + support keywords with high engagement, (c) news mentions of [company] + "customer service" / "support failure" / "data breach", (d) regulatory complaint trackers for B2C-heavy verticals.

Enrich. Function 2 (Persona Mapper) immediately, targeting the senior-most CX leader at the company.

Transform. Auto-route as URGENT if the failure is within 7 days. These are 24-hour-response signals, not weekly-digest signals.

Create. Outreach is delicate: never reference the failure directly (corporate sensitivity); reference the category. "I saw the conversation about [topic] this week. Most CX teams I work with are getting asked by their CEOs how to prevent the next one. Solidroad does X for [similar company]. Worth a 15-min conversation?"

Signal 5 · CX hiring wave (10+ support roles in 30 days)

The hypothesis. Companies hiring 10+ support agents in a 30-day window are scaling, which means onboarding is about to break and time-to-quality is a board-visible problem. Faire's 33% onboarding reduction is the proof case to lead with.

Find. Default signal: job postings. Filter for support / CX / customer success roles. Aggregate by company over rolling 30-day windows.

Enrich. Function 1 (Account ICP). Particularly: which support stack are they on? Intercom and Zendesk are warm – Solidroad integrates deeply with both.

Transform. Score by hiring velocity + ICP fit + stack compatibility. Hiring 10 agents on Intercom in 30 days = call this week.

Create. "Saw you're hiring [N] support roles in [city/region]. Faire ran into the same growth shape – they cut onboarding time 33% by [Solidroad capability]. Curious if you're feeling the same pressure?"

Signal 6 · BPO client win or RFP announcement

The hypothesis. BPOs (Tech Mahindra, Crescendo-tier) win new client engagements all the time. When they do, they need to demonstrate quality infrastructure to the new client. Solidroad's pitch lands as a "use this in your client pitch" angle, not a "buy this for your ops" angle. BPOs are also unlocks: one BPO win can mean 10+ underlying client logos.

Find. RSS feed monitoring of BPO press releases + LinkedIn announcements about new client wins. Filter for B2C-heavy verticals where Solidroad has case studies (e-commerce, fintech, travel).

Enrich. Function 1 + Function 2 targeting BPO operations leaders (COO, VP Operations, Account Director on the specific new account).

Transform. Score boost: BPO + new client win in last 60 days + ICP-aligned underlying client.

Create. "Saw [BPO] just won the [new client] account. The Tech Mahindra team uses Solidroad to lead with their quality story in client pitches – worth showing you the angle before kickoff?"

Signal 7 · Multi-language / international CX expansion

The hypothesis. International expansion = new languages = new agent cohorts = calibration drift across regions. Crescendo's 50% manual-assessment reduction across Honduras + Philippines is the proof case. The pain is acute and well-named.

Find. Combined signal: (a) job postings for support agents specifying new languages (Spanish, Portuguese, Tagalog, etc.) + (b) press releases about new market entry + (c) careers-page localisation changes.

Enrich. Function 1 + Function 2 targeting Director of CX Operations or Head of International Operations.

Transform. Score boost: 3+ international support roles in 30 days OR explicit market-entry announcement.

Create. "Saw you're expanding into [region]. Crescendo runs Solidroad across their Honduras and Philippines ops to keep quality consistent across sites – cut manual assessment 50%. Same shape of problem worth a conversation?"

Signal 8 · Competitor renewal window (MaestroQA, Level AI, Convin)

The hypothesis. The strongest displacement deals come 60–90 days before a competitor contract renewal, when the buyer is open to alternatives. Most competitor signals are public-ish: G2 reviews dated by date of purchase, LinkedIn posts mentioning current tools, support job descriptions listing the QA stack.

Find. Claygent monitoring of (a) public G2 / TrustRadius reviews mentioning competitor names + dates + (b) job postings listing competitor tools in the stack + (c) Glassdoor reviews mentioning frustration with current tools.

Enrich. Function 1 + Function 2. Particularly the CX ops leader's tenure – longer tenure = stronger personal authority to switch vendors.

Transform. Score boost: competitor in stack + renewal window approaching + recent CX leadership change (the new leader didn't pick the old tool and has cover to switch).

Create. Never trash the competitor directly. "I noticed you're on [competitor]. We're built differently around the simulation-to-coaching loop, which is where [similar customer] told us they hit a ceiling. Worth showing you the difference before your next renewal cycle?"

Signal 9 · Series B+ funding in CX-heavy verticals

The hypothesis. Newly funded companies in e-commerce, fintech, marketplaces, and consumer apps have budget, growth pressure, and CX is one of the first functions that breaks at scale. Series B is the sweet spot – Series A is too early (no CX team yet), Series C is too late (already chose a vendor).

Find. Default signal: funding announcements. Filter for Series B + CX-heavy verticals.

Enrich. Function 1 (Account ICP). Function 2 targeting VP Customer Support or first CX hire if no leader yet (also a wedge – we can help them shape the function from day one).

Transform. Score boost: Series B + B2C / marketplace / fintech + already deploying AI agents.

Create. "Congrats on the Series B. Most companies at your stage feel CX scaling break around the 50-agent mark. Faire ran into that and we [outcome]. Curious if you're seeing the same."

Signal 10 · CX conference speaker / panelist

The hypothesis. CX leaders speaking at Support Driven, Customer Contact Week, ICMI, etc. are publicly committing to a position on a CX topic. That's the perfect cold-outreach hook – reference their specific talk, ask the question their talk implicitly raises, position Solidroad as the answer.

Find. RSS feed + manual maintained list of CX conference speaker pages. Claygent scrape of event sites monthly.

Enrich. Function 2 + a custom Claygent pull of the talk abstract / video / LinkedIn post about the talk.

Transform. Score boost: ICP fit + recent public talk + talk topic aligns with Solidroad capability (AI agents, QA, training, multi-language).

Create. "Saw your talk at [conference] on [topic]. You said [specific quote]. That's exactly the gap we close at Solidroad – worth comparing notes for 20 minutes?"

03 · what i'd kill from the list, deliberately

The signals I'm not chasing.

  • Generic intent data from third-party providers (Bombora, G2 Buyer Intent). Late-stage by definition – by the time someone shows up on a buyer-intent list, they're already evaluating MaestroQA. We want earlier.
  • Tech stack signals for non-CX tools. A company adopting a new finance tool tells us nothing about their CX posture. Focus is a feature, not a bug.
  • Volume-based job-posting signals not specific to CX. Engineering hiring waves are not Solidroad signals. Resist the temptation to over-broaden.
  • Press mentions of "AI" without context. Half of corporate America is now mentioning AI in press releases. Without specificity about AI in customer support, it's noise.

The discipline is to monitor fewer signals more deeply, score them rigorously, and act on the ones that score 80+. A team running 30 signals badly will be outperformed by a team running 10 signals well.
