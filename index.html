A note on the product alignment

Solidroad's pitch is the insight-to-action gap.

Mark's been writing publicly about it for months. The take-home applies the same shape to GTM.

Solidroad for CX

Most CX teams have insight (scoring, dashboards, samples). Few have action (training that actually fixes the gap). Solidroad closes the loop from gap detection to coaching simulation, automatically.

This system for GTM

Most outbound teams have signals (Clay enrichments, intent data). Few have action (the system that converts signals into pipeline at lower cost than SDRs). This take-home closes the same loop, applied to outbound.

Why the alignment matters. The same insight-to-action discipline Solidroad applies to CX is the discipline this take-home applies to GTM. The operator role isn't just outbound at scale – it's the loop-closer between signal and pipeline, in the same way Solidroad is the loop-closer between scoring and coaching.
